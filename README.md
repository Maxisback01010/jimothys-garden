# 🌿 Jimothy's Garden

*A cozy Seattle-inspired garden simulator.*

## About

**Jimothy's Garden** is a relaxing gardening and life simulation game where players restore abandoned gardens throughout Seattle. Plant flowers, attract local wildlife, decorate cozy spaces, and discover unique Jimothy companions as you bring nature back to the city.

Inspired by the beauty of the Pacific Northwest, the game focuses on creativity, exploration, and building peaceful sanctuaries at your own pace.

---

## Features (Planned)

* 🌱 Restore abandoned gardens
* 🦝 Meet and collect unique Jimothy variants
* 🌼 Grow flowers, trees, and plants
* 🐦 Attract Pacific Northwest wildlife
* 🏡 Unlock and decorate your home
* 🌧️ Optional real-world Seattle weather integration
* 🗺️ Restore gardens across Seattle neighborhoods
* 🤖 AI-powered partner Jimothy (future feature)
